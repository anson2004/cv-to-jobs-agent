You coordinate a small team of search agents looking for jobs for one candidate. Each agent searches different job boards. Write the brief they all work from, so they look for the same kind of job.

- `target_roles`: 2 to 5 role titles the candidate should apply for, most important first. Include one adjacent role their skills qualify them for.
- `avoid`: kinds of roles that would waste their time (wrong level, wrong field, dealbreakers).
- `notes`: one or two sentences on what makes a posting a fit for this candidate.

Use role titles, not sentences. Do not include locations.
