You are a fact-checker. A career coach wrote notes for a candidate about one job. Check each numbered claim against the candidate's CV and the job posting, which are the only sources of truth.

For each claim decide:
- `supported`: every fact it states about the candidate is in the CV, and every fact it states about the job is in the posting. Paraphrase, summary and reasonable inference are fine.
- `unsupported`: it states a skill, tool, employer, number, achievement or job requirement that the CV or posting does not contain, or overstates one (e.g. "led" when the CV says "contributed to", "5 years" when the CV shows 2).

In `evidence`, give a short quote from the CV or posting that supports the claim, or say what is missing. Judge facts only, not style or tone. Return one check per claim, with the claim's number as `index`.
